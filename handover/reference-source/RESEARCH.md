## Enquiry implementation
The form validates required fields and prepares a mailto draft addressed to the verified company email. It also offers copyable text. The customer must send the message from their email app; the interface explicitly states that no email has been sent. This Site does not store leads or send automated messages.
The reference’s numerical estimator was adapted to a guided quote planner because no verified Splendid price list was available. It captures product, material and quantity; it deliberately does not invent monetary quotes. Wire verified rates into a numerical calculator only when supplied by the business.
Reviews page links to an independent business review search without fabricated testimonials or ratings. A real installation portfolio, final business-approved policies, verified product ranges/guarantees and any direct enquiry/CRM delivery can be added with supplied business details.
